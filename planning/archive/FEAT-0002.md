# Feature (FEAT)

Title: FEAT-0002 — Backtesting harness and metrics
Status: approved
Owner: Wilson Reyes
Related: BUS-0001, SYS-0001, TECH-0001, ADR-0001

## Context
Provide fast, reliable backtesting with walk-forward and OOS evaluation and standard trading metrics.

## Scope
- In scope
  - Vectorized backtester with slippage/spread and order constraints (rate caps, exposure)
  - Metrics: Sharpe, Sortino, CAGR, MDD, Calmar, hit rate, avg win/loss, turnover
  - Protocols: time-series CV, walk-forward, OOS holdout; leakage checks
- Out of scope
  - Portfolio optimization (later)

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given strategy signals and 1m OHLCV, when run, then metrics are reported and persisted with lineage
- Given protocol settings, when executed, then OOS results meet thresholds or fail clearly

## Non-Functional Needs
- Throughput to evaluate ~100 strategies/day
- Deterministic runs: seeds, code hash, dataset version logged

## Dependencies
- TECH-0001 (storage/config)

## Risks & Controls
- Overfitting → enforced OOS and leakage checks
