# Feature (FEAT)

Title: FEAT-0004 — Risk configuration and circuit breaker
Status: approved
Owner: Wilson Reyes
Related: BUS-0001, SYS-0001, ADR-0001

## Context
Implement enforceable risk limits and circuit-breaker behavior per MVP constraints.

## Scope
- In scope
  - Central policy engine: position sizing, exposure caps, order-rate limit, session window
  - Circuit breaker: trip on daily loss, consecutive errors, latency breach
  - Configurable via typed configs; environment separated
- Out of scope
  - News/event risk filters (later)

## Acceptance Criteria
- Orders violating policy are denied with reason; limits observable in metrics
- Circuit breaker trips produce alerts and halt trading as configured

## Non-Functional Needs
- Policy p99 < 50ms

## Dependencies
- SYS-0001 (pipeline), MVP constraints

## Risks & Controls
- Misconfiguration → defaults safe-to-flat; config validation

## Compliance
- [ ] Architecture Principles reviewed
- [ ] NFRs impact assessed
- [ ] Security Constraints verified

## Validation Plan
- TEST-0004: simulate breaches and verify halts/alerts
