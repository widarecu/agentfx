# Feature (FEAT)

Title: FEAT-0005 — Paper trading runner
Status: approved
Owner: Wilson Reyes
Related: BUS-0001, SYS-0001, FEAT-0004, ADR-0001

## Context
Run strategies in a paper environment with real-time data to validate E2E behavior. Target broker: OANDA Practice.

## Scope
- In scope
  - Connect to broker/data feed; simulate fills with spread+slippage model
  - Reconcile orders/positions; record decision provenance
  - Feature flags to enable/disable strategies; environment toggles
- Out of scope
  - Live capital execution (later)

## Acceptance Criteria
- Strategy runs in paper ≥ 2 weeks and meets thresholds; decision logs complete

## Non-Functional Needs
- Paper loop p95 < 1s; reliable retries; audit trails

## Dependencies
- FEAT-0002, FEAT-0004, SYS-0001

## Risks & Controls
- Slippage misestimation → compare modeled vs observed; adjust later

- [ ] Architecture Principles reviewed
- [ ] NFRs impact assessed
- [ ] Security Constraints verified

## Validation Plan
- TEST-0005: dry-run with mock broker; soak test in paper
