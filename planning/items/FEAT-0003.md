# Feature (FEAT)

Title: FEAT-0003 — Baseline strategy templates
Status: proposed
Owner: Wilson Reyes
Related: BUS-0001, FEAT-0002, TECH-0001

## Context
Provide starter strategies for evaluation and for the autonomous generator to mutate.

## Scope
- In scope
  - Momentum (MA crossover), mean-reversion (RSI/bands), breakout (ATR/Donchian)
  - Unified interface: fit/predict/signal; risk hooks for sizing/SL/TP
- Out of scope
  - ML-based models (later in autonomy phase)

## Acceptance Criteria
- Each template runs in backtester, emits valid orders under constraints, and produces metrics

## Non-Functional Needs
- Clear configuration; reproducible defaults

## Dependencies
- FEAT-0002 (backtester), TECH-0001

## Risks & Controls
- Data-snooping → fixed parameter presets for baselines

- [ ] Architecture Principles reviewed
- [ ] NFRs impact assessed
- [ ] Security Constraints verified
